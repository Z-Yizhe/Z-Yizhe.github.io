# Numerical Methods
## 1.basic defenitions:
### 1.polynomial interpolation:
if we have n+1 points ininterval[a,b]:  
  
$$
a \leq x_0 \lt x_1 \lt \cdots \lt x_n \leq b
$$

and $p(x_i)=y_i,i=0,1...n$   
the progress finding the polynomial is called apolynomial interpolation.
## 2.special situations:  
### 1:linear interpolation:  (n=1)
For $y_1=f(x_1),y_2=f(x_2)$ ,if we need to find a polynomial that satisfies this condition,so we can find:

$$
\begin{cases}
L_1(x)=y=y_0+\frac{y_1-y_0}{x_1-x_0}(x-x_0)（点斜式）\\  
\frac{y-y_0}{y_1-y_0}=\frac{x-x_0}{x_1-x_0}（两点式)
\end{cases}
$$

we can find that:

$$
L_1(x)={y_0}{l_0(x)}+{y_1}{l_1(x)} 
$$

in which:

$$
l_0(x)=\frac{x-x_1}{x_0-x_1},l_1(x)=\frac{x-x_0}{x_1-x_0} 
$$

we can also find taht:

$$
l_0(x_0)=1,l_0(x_1)=0,   l_1(x_0)=0,l_1(x_1)=1
$$

We call these kinds of functions **linear intepolation basis function**.

### 2.parabolic interpolation polynomial    (n=2)

Now consider more complicated cases:  

$$
\begin{cases}
l_{k-1}(x_{k-1})=1,      l_{k-1}(x_j)=0,  \qquad     j=k,k+1\\
l_{k}(x_{k})=1,      l_{k}(x_j)=0 , \qquad j=k-1,k+1\\
l_{k+1}(x_{k+1})=1,      l_{k-1}(x_j)=0, \qquad  j=k-1,k.
\end{cases}
$$

It's not hard to find $l_j=\frac{(x-x_{x_1})(x-x_{x_2})}{(x_j-x_{x_1})(x_j-x_{x_2})} \qquad (j \neq x_1,x_2)$  
and $L_2(x)=y_{k-1}l_{k-1}+y_kl_k+y_{k+1}l_{k+1}$ can be calculated imediately.  
we call $L_2$--parabolic interpolation polynomial.

### 3 General case

for $ L_n(x_j)=y_j$,if we have such polynomials:
$$
l_j(x_i)=\begin{cases}
          0 \qquad if i \neq j\\
          1 \qquad if i=j
          \end{cases}
$$
