# 数项级数
## 1 数项级数的敛散性
### 1.basic defenition:
We call sum of countably infinitely many real numbers **series**.  
Introduce the notation:
  
$$
\sum_{i=0}^{n} x_i
$$

And $x_n$  --**general term**.  

We call $S_N= \sum_{i=1}^{N} x_i$ ---the **N-th partial sum of the series**.  
And if the sequence $S_n$ is convergent,then we could say that **the series is convergent**.  
Else we call it **the divergent sequence**.

### 2.1 special situations:
1. if |q|<1,then $\sum_{i=1}^{n} q^{n-1}$ is convergent,and

$$
\lim_{n \to \infty} Sn= \frac{1}{1-q}.
$$

2. the series

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{ \infty } \frac{1}{n^{p}}
$$

is convergent,when p>1.and we can construct the **sequence of remainders** $r_n$:

$$
r_n=\sum_{k=n+1}^{\infty}a_k.
$$

Obviously,it is now convergent.
### 2.2 basic properties of series:

1.if series $\sum{i=1}^{n}xn$ is convergent,then $\lim_{n \to \infty}x_n=0$.   
**proof**:

$$
x_n=S_n-S_{n-1}.
$$

Letting $x \to \infty$ gives the desired result.  
<br>
<br>
<br>

2. if $\sum_{i=1}^{ \infty}a_i=A,\sum_{i=1}^{ \infty}b_i=B$,then:  

$$
\sum_{i=1}^{n}(\alpha a_n+\beta b_n)=\alpha A+\beta B.
$$

**proof**:

$$
S_n=\alpha S^{1}_n+ \beta S^{2}_n
$$

Letting $x \to \infty$ gives the desired result.

3.Convergent series satisfy the associative law.<br>
<br>
**proof**:
If $\sum_{i=0}^{ \infty}y_n=(x_1+x_2+...+x_{n_1})+\cdots+(x_{n_{k-1}+1}+\cdots+{x_{n_k}}+\cdots$  

Introduce the notations:<br>
$U_k$--partial  sum  of $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}y_n$,and $S_{n_k}$ --partial sum of $\sum_{i=0}^{\infty}x_n$.
then we can find that $U_n is the subsequence of S_n$,so $U_n$ is also convergent,and the limit is same as
$S_n.\qquad$   **Q.E.D**
