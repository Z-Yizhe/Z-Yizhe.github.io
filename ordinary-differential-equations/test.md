# Ordinary Differential Equations

## Test of LaTeX

Consider the differential equation

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+P(x)y=Q(x).
$$

Its integrating factor is

$$
\mu(x)=e^{\int P(x)\,dx}.
$$

Therefore,

$$
y=e^{-\int P(x)\,dx}
\left(
\int Q(x)e^{\int P(x)\,dx}\,dx+C
\right).
$$

We can also write an inline formula such as $y'=f(x,y)$.
