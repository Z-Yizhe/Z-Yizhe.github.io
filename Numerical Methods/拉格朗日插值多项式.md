## 1.special situations:  
### 1:linear interpolation:  
for $y_1=f(x_1),y_2=f(x_2)$ ,we need to find a polynomial that satisfies this condition,so we can find:

$$
\begin{cases}
L_1(x)=y=y_0+\frac{y_1-y_0}{x_1-x_0}(x-x_0)（点斜式）\\  
\frac{y-y_0}{y_1-y_0}=\frac{x-x_0}{x_1-x_0}（两点式)
\end{cases}
$$

we can find that:

$$
L_1(x)={y_0}{l_0(x)}+{y_1}{l_1(x)} 

l_0(x)=\frac{x-x_1}{x_0-x_1},l_1(x)=\frac{x-x_0}{x_1-x_0} 
$$
