# ODEs
## 初等变换法
### 1.Why we use such a method.
We have already learned 3 kinds of equations--seperatable differential equations,first-order linear equations and exact differential equations,with **elementary transformation method**,we can <br>expand the scope of equations that we can solve.<br>
### 2. example:
2.1: for equations like:

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=f(x+y)
$$

Letting u=x+y ,then we have:

$$
\frac{du}{dx}=1+f(u)
$$

Now it can be seen as a seperatable differential equation.

### 3.special types:<br>
Now consider 3 types of special equations:  
<br>
#### 1.homogeneous equation:<br>
<br>
for equations like:

$$
P(x,y)dx+Q(x,y)dy=0
$$

if $P(tx,ty)=t^{m}P(x,y),Q(tx,ty)=t^(m)Q(x,y)$,then we call the equation **homogeneous equation**.

A principle is:  
    y=ux<br>
then we have:

$$
x^{m}[p(1,u)+uQ(1,u)]dx+x^{m+1}Q(1,u)du=0.
$$

Which can be seen as as a seperatable differential equation.

**Note 1:** another defeniton is that the equation can be simplified to :

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}=\Phi(\frac{dy}{dx})
$$
<br>
**Note 2:** x=0 is a solution of the equation$x^{m}[p(1,u)+uQ(1,u)]dx+x^{m+1}Q(1,u)du=0.$,but   
it might not be a solution of the primary equation,as the process y=ux is not invertible when x=0.<br>

#### 2. Bernoulli differential equation
equations like 

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+p(x)y=q(x)y^{n}
$$

are called Bernoulli differential equations,while n!=0,1.<br>
Multiply $(1-n)y^{-n}$ on both sides of the equation,we have:

$$
(1-n)y^{-n}\frac{dy}{dx}+(1-n)y^{1-n}p(x)=(1-n)q(x).
$$

Introduce the notation:

$$
z=y^{1-n}
$$

Then we have:

$$
\frac{dz}{dx}+(1-n)p(x)z=(1-n)q(x)
$$

And that is a first-order linear differential equation.

#### 3.Riccati equation
equations like

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+p(x)y=f(x,y)
$$

are called Riccati equations,where $f(x,y)=p(x)y^{2}+q(x)y+r(x)$
p(x),q(x) and r(x) are continious on the interval,while $p(x) \not\equiv 0$

**theorem 1** if $y= \Phi(x)$ as a specific solution is known,then we can find its general solution with integration method.<br>
<br>
**proof**:make a transformation: $y=u+ \Phi(x)$,where u can be seen as a new function,substitute it into the equation,we can find:

$$
\frac{du}{dx}=[2p(x)(\Phi(x))+q(x)]u+p(x)u^{2}.
$$

This is a Bernoulli differential equation,so the general solution can be found.

**theorem 2**:if the Riccati equation is  

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+ay^{2}=bx^{m}
$$
where a,b,m are constants,a!=0,and if $x,y \neq 0$,if m=

$$
0,-2,\frac{-4k}{2k+1},\frac{-4k}{2k-1}
$$

then we can find a general solution as the simplified equation is turned into separatable differential equation.
