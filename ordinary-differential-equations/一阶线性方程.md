# ODEs
## 1.basic defenitions:    
Consider such equation:  

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+p(x)y=q(x)
$$

it is called a **first-order linear differential equation**.

## 2.method of solution
### 2.1 (q(x)=0):
if q(x)=0,then it is a separatable differential equation:

$$
\frac{dy}{y}=-p(x)dx
$$

the general integral is

$$
-\int p(x)dx=lny+c
$$

so the general solution is:

$$
y=Ce^{-\int p(x)dx}
$$

###2.2 (q(x)!=0):
then we have the following form:

$$
dy+p(x)dx=q(x)dx
$$

Multiply both sides by the integrating factor $u(x)= e^{\int p(x)dx}$,we have:

$$
e^{\int p(x)dx}dy+e^{\int p(x)dx}p(x)ydx=e^{\int p(x)dx}q(x)dx\\
$$

so we can have:  

$$
d(e^{\int p(x)dx}y)=d \int q(x)e^{\int p(x)dx}dx
$$

that is

$$
e^{\int p(x)dx}y=\int q(x)e^{\int p(x)dx}dx + C
$$

And this is the general solution of the equation.
