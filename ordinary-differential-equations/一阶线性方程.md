# ODEs
## 1.basic defenitions:    
Consider such equation:  

$$
\frac{dy}{dx}+p(x)y=q(x)
$$

it is called a **first-order linear differential equation**.

## 2.method of solution
### 2.1 (q(x)=0):
if q(x)=0,then it is a separatable differential equation:

$$
\frac{dy}{y}=-p(x)dx
$$

the general integral is

$$
-\int p(x)dx=lny+c
$$

so the general solution is:

$$
y=Ce^{-\int p(x)dx}
$$

### 2.2 (q(x)!=0):
then we have the following form:

$$
dy+p(x)dx=q(x)dx
$$

Multiply both sides by the integrating factor $u(x)= e^{\int p(x)dx}$,we have:

$$
e^{\int p(x)dx}dy+e^{\int p(x)dx}p(x)ydx=e^{\int p(x)dx}q(x)dx\\
$$

so we can have:  

$$
d(e^{\int p(x)dx}y)=d \int q(x)e^{\int p(x)dx}dx
$$

that is

$$
e^{\int p(x)dx}y=\int q(x)e^{\int p(x)dx}dx + C
$$

And this is the general solution of the equation.  

## 3.basic properties
1.the solution of homogeneous equation is either identically equal to 0,either identically not equal to 0.
2.Any solution exists on the whole interval I,where p(x) and q(x) exist and are continuous.
3.linear combination of solution of homogeneous equation is still his solution; linear combination of homogeneous and nonhomogeneous equations are solution of nonhomogeneous equations;<br>
The difference between any solutions of a nonhomogeneous equation is the solution of corresponding homogeneous equation.<br>
4.Sum of general solution of homogeneous equation and arbitrary solution of nonhomogeneous equation compose the general solution of the nonhomogeneous equation.
5.solution of initial value problem of linear equations exists and is unique.
